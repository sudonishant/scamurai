# Legal Disclaimer & Evidentiary Notice

Scamurai is an open-source technical investigation and analytical workspace designed for researchers, digital forensics analysts, and open-source intelligence (OSINT) practitioners.

## 1. No Legal Advice
The outputs, dossiers, metrics, and exports generated by Scamurai do NOT constitute legal advice or formal expert testimony. Scamurai is a software platform, not a legal practice or accredited forensic laboratory.

## 2. Indian Evidence Act Section 65B & BSA 2023 Section 63
The Law Enforcement Agency (LEA) Dossier export format models the technical provenance and chain-of-custody metadata traditionally referenced in Indian Evidence Act Section 65B (and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 Section 63) proceedings.

Generating this document does **NOT** by itself make any electronic record legally admissible in a court of law. Legal admissibility requires:
- Compliance with the mandatory statutory conditions under Section 65B(2) / Section 63(2);
- Execution of an accompanying certificate signed by the authorized lawful custodian under Section 65B(4) / Section 63(4);
- Unbroken chain of custody verified by qualified forensic examiners and legal counsel.
